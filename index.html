I'm an MSc student in AI at IMT Nord Europe, currently on exchange at GIST in 🇰🇷. I mostly work on embodied AI: world models, representation learning, multimodal perception and human-robot interaction.

  • At GIST, I'm building an action-conditioned world model for collision avoidance with Florigen Robotics, trained on synthetic Isaac Sim data.
  • At Enchanted Tools, I built turn-taking, barge-in and backchanneling for Mirokaï, a cute social humanoid. Basically, teaching it when to talk and when to shut up and listen. This led to a NeurIPS 2026 workshop paper.
  • Through Algoverse research fellowship, I worked on robustness of tokenizers and VLMs in a medical context wich also led to a NeurIPS 2026 workshop paper.
  • Before that, I shipped on-device TTS and RAG on an autonomous vehicle at Ferromobile.

I'm drawn to AI that has to survive contact with the real world: perceive, predict, act, and not fall over, ideally where it genuinely helps people. I'm looking for a 6-month research internship starting between January and March 2027, before a PhD in fall 2027. Feel free to reach out ^^ !
