I'm an MSc student in AI at IMT Nord Europe, currently on exchange at GIST in South Korea 🇰🇷. I work at the intersection of embodied AI, representation learning and multimodal perception, with a focus on world models and on understanding when learned representations break.

I've worked in pretty different setups, from shipping models on real hardware to academic research:

At Enchanted Tools, I worked on the brain of Mirokaï, a social humanoid robot: turn-taking, user barge-in and backchanneling. Mostly teaching a robot when to talk and when to shut up and listen. Part of this work turned into a paper accepted at a NeurIPS 2026 workshop.

At Ferromobile, I deployed an on-device TTS system and a local RAG pipeline on an autonomous vehicle platform. Embedded hardware has strong opinions about your latency budget.

Through the Algoverse research program, I led a first-author project on adversarial attacks against discrete medical image tokenizers, accepted at two NeurIPS 2026 workshops, including the GenAI4Health main track.

At GIST, I'm building an action-conditioned world model for collision avoidance with a robotics startup, while taking courses in 3D vision and robot control.

I'm drawn to AI that has to survive contact with the real world. Anything that has to perceive, act, and not fall over. I'm looking for a 6-month research internship starting January to March 2027, before a PhD in fall 2027, ideally on work that reaches people in healthcare or assistive robotics. Feel free to reach out if you want to chat!
